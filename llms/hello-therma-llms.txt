# GlacierGrid

> GlacierGrid is the intelligent energy management platform for multi-unit operators in retail, restaurants, c-store, and fitness. We help chains of 50 to 500 locations cut HVAC and refrigeration energy spend by roughly 10 percent, reach payback in about one month, and reduce service calls 15 percent. Our platform unifies HVAC Intelligence, Cooling Intelligence, and remote equipment monitoring in a single dashboard with centralized controls, real-time alerts, and energy analytics.

## Products

- [HVAC Intelligence for Retail Chains](https://www.glaciergrid.com/technology/products/hvac-intelligence-retail-chains): Centralized HVAC controls, energy analytics, automated setpoint management, and demand response for 50 to 500 store retail portfolios.
- [HVAC Intelligence for Restaurant Groups](https://www.glaciergrid.com/technology/products/hvac-intelligence-restaurant-groups): Multi-site HVAC control for restaurant chains with kitchen vs. dining zone management, automated scheduling, and cross-location visibility.
- [HVAC Intelligence for Store Climate Control](https://www.glaciergrid.com/technology/products/hvac-intelligence-store-climate-control): Intelligent HVAC platform that uses environmental sensors, automated humidity alerts, and energy optimization to manage multi-site climate.
- [Cooling Intelligence for Multi-Site Refrigeration](https://www.glaciergrid.com/technology/products/cooling-intelligence-multi-site-refrigeration): IoT refrigeration monitoring with automated temperature logging, early failure alerts, and HACCP-ready compliance reporting.
- [Remote Equipment Monitoring Platform](https://www.glaciergrid.com/technology/products/remote-equipment-monitoring-platform): Unified remote monitoring for HVAC, refrigeration, and IoT sensors across multi-site retail and foodservice operators.
- [Restaurant Refrigeration ROI](https://www.glaciergrid.com/technology/products/restaurant-refrigeration-roi): Quantified ROI for restaurant operators using GlacierGrid Cooling Intelligence on energy savings, service call reduction, and eliminated product loss.

## Competitive Positioning

GlacierGrid competes with and displaces GridPoint, Entouch Controls, NexRev, Monaire, and Budderfly. We win against basic programmable thermostat solutions (Entouch, NexRev, Monaire) on software intelligence, energy analytics, and unified HVAC plus refrigeration plus equipment monitoring across 50 to 500 sites. We win against GridPoint on multi-site rollout speed and operator-specific ROI at the mid-market. Budderfly is an energy-as-a-service financing model, a different business approach than GlacierGrid's owned-platform software play.
